Company Description

About Oscorp Biosciences

Oscorp Biosciences develops enzyme therapies and diagnostic kits for rare metabolic disorders. Our labs, plants and offices run on a shared set of business systems, and this team keeps those systems honest, connected and easy to use.

Job Description

As an Analyst on the Business Systems team you will sit between the people who use our enterprise software every day and the engineers who configure it. You will learn how orders, batches, shipments and invoices move through the company, write down what each group needs, and turn that into changes we can build, test and release without surprises.

You will spend most of your week with finance, supply chain and quality partners: gathering requirements, mapping current and future processes, writing user stories and acceptance criteria, and walking teams through what changed and why. The rest goes to testing, data checks and tidying up the documentation nobody else wants to own.

Key Responsibilities

  • Run discovery sessions with business partners and capture requirements as clear user stories
  • Map end-to-end processes across order-to-cash, procure-to-pay and batch release, and spot gaps between systems
  • Write functional specifications and test scripts, then lead user acceptance testing with the people who will use the change
  • Triage incoming requests, size them with the engineering team and keep the backlog groomed and visible
  • Investigate data issues between the ERP, the warehouse system and the quality management system, and follow them to a fix
  • Prepare release notes, training guides and short demos so each change lands smoothly
  • Support month-end and audit activities with reports and evidence from the systems you look after
  • Keep validated systems in a compliant state by following change control and documenting every step

Qualifications

Required
  • Bachelor's degree in information systems, business, engineering or a related field
  • Zero to two years in business analysis, systems support or a similar role; internships count
  • Comfort with spreadsheets and basic SQL for checking data
  • Clear writing and the patience to explain a system to someone who has never seen it
  • Interest in regulated industries and why their rules exist
Preferred
  • Exposure to an ERP system and to change control in a validated environment
  • Experience writing test cases or running user acceptance tests
  • Familiarity with a ticketing tool and an agile way of working

Additional Information
