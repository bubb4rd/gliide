Aperture Wearables is hiring a Software Engineer to build quick, dependable web experiences for our line of AI glasses and the pocket hub that runs their models on device.

You will own the web side of launch: the product site, the store and checkout, preorder drops, customer accounts and the companion dashboard.


What You’ll Work On

  1. Ship responsive web apps in React, Next.js and TypeScript that stay fast on slow phones
  2. Build checkout, preorder and account flows with our payments and fulfillment partners
  3. Instrument pages and fix what the numbers show, from load time to drop-off
  4. Work with design and firmware on the pairing flow between web and device


What You Bring

  • A degree in computer science or equivalent experience; new graduates welcome
  • Comfort with HTML, CSS & modern JavaScript
  • Curiosity about hardware & on-device AI
