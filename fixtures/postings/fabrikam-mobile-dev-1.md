What is Fabrikam Health? 

 Fabrikam Health makes the on-call and paging software that hospital care teams carry on their phones. Founded in 2004, we've grown into a team of 300+ people serving more than 2,000 hospitals and clinics, and our apps route millions of messages, pages and alerts every week.

Fabrikam Health’s mission is simple: get the right message to the right clinician the first time. When a lab result comes back critical at 3 a.m., our software knows who is on call, finds them, and makes sure someone answers — so nurses and doctors spend less time chasing each other and more time with patients.

What our customers say about us:

- In 2026, hospital IT leaders ranked Fabrikam Health first for reliability in the Example Health IT Survey — for the third year running.

- Our mobile app holds a 4.8 rating from clinicians on both app stores.

 At Fabrikam Health, you'll join a team that has done this for a long time and still argues about how to do it better. We're adding AI to scheduling and message routing, testing new workflows with customers, and rebuilding parts of our apps from the ground up. Wherever you sit, you'll work on things that matter to people at the bedside.

If you want a stable company with modern tech and people who care about the work, read on!

 The ideal candidate is early in their mobile development career but already writes solid code and has shipped something with React Native. You'll be comfortable on a cross-functional team, building features for both iOS and Android from one codebase, and happy to switch between UI work, integration work and debugging as priorities shift.

 Responsibilities

- Build and maintain our cross-platform mobile app in React Native for iOS and Android, guided by senior mobile engineers.

- Turn UI/UX designs and wireframes into clean, responsive code.

- Connect the app to RESTful APIs and third-party SDKs.

- Find and fix bugs, slowdowns, and differences between iOS and Android.

- Take part in code reviews and act on feedback from senior engineers.

- Write and maintain basic unit and integration tests that keep the app stable.

- Communicate well on a remote team, with your camera on in meetings when expected.

 Qualifications

- Some prior technical experience in mobile development, software development, or a related technical area
 OR a bachelor's degree in computer science, Software Engineering, or another programming/technology-related field from an accredited higher education institution.

- Working knowledge of React Native and JavaScript/TypeScript, including React fundamentals such as components, state, props, and hooks.

- Basic understanding of the software development lifecycle (SDLC) and/or Agile delivery practices.

- Familiarity with mobile UI/UX principles and an interest in growing on both iOS and Android.

- Experience using Git for version control and consuming REST APIs.

- Strong communication skills and the ability to work well with cross-functional teams.

- Health IT experience (a plus)
