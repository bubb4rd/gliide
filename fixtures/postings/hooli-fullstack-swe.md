# Software Engineer (Full-Stack)

## Location

Remote - USA

## Employment Type

Full time

## Location Type

Remote

## Department

Engineering

Hooli Cloud rents GPUs by the second to teams training and serving AI models. Started in 2021, we're a fast-growing, remote-first company with engineers on four continents. We want running a model in production to feel as easy as running it on your laptop.

We're hiring a Full-Stack Engineer. As a Full-Stack Software Engineer at Hooli Cloud, you'll work next to experienced engineers on the console, APIs and developer tools that sit between AI teams and our GPU fleet. You'll learn fast and see your work in customers' hands within weeks.

**Responsibilities:**

***Build Across the Frontend & Backend:**** Help build and improve the console, public APIs and core services of our platform in Python, Typescript, and Go Lang (mostly Python and Typescript).****Own Parts of the SDK:**** Keep our open-source SDK simple and dependable for developers deploying on Hooli Cloud.****Work With Engineering & AI Teams:**** Ship features and fix bugs that make life easier for the AI engineers and researchers who use the platform.****Testing & Code Quality:**** Write clean, fast, maintainable code, with good tests and continuous integration.****Keep Learning:**** Join code reviews and pairing sessions, and grow your full-stack skills.*

**Requirements:**
- ***Programming Skills:**** Some experience with Go Lang and/or Typescript, and a wish to grow in a full-stack role.*
- ***Web Development Knowledge: ****Strong experience with frontend technologies (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript frameworks such as React) and backend frameworks (e.g., Express, FastAPI), plus libraries such as Pydantic.*
- ***Open Source Interest:**** Open-source contributions are a strong plus.*
- ***Problem-Solving Skills:**** You like hard problems, take feedback well, and work well with others.*
- ***Communication Skills:**** You can explain technical ideas to developers and to non-technical teammates.*
- Successful completion of a background check

**Preferred:**
- *Exposure to cloud platforms, distributed computing, or containers such as Docker.*
- *Familiarity with modern engineering practices.*
- *Coursework or projects that show full-stack work.*
- *Experience with Git and version control workflows.*

**What You’ll Receive:**
- The base pay for this position ranges from ($125,000 - $190,000). The range spans more than one level and narrows during interviews based on experience, skills and location
- Equity for everyone on the team — when the company grows, you share in it.
- Medical, dental & vision plans
- Flexible PTO- rest when you need to
- Remote-first teams that work mostly in writing, over chat and docs
- A team that cares about learning and ownership
- $1,000 Home Office & Equipment Stipend- set up your workspace the way you like it
