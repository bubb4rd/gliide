## UX Engineer

Oak Brook, IL

$98,000 ‒ $104,000 Annually

**You turn good ideas into web experiences people can actually use. You want a role where front-end development, CMS work, UX thinking and quick experiments all meet, and where you'll work with members, editors, marketers and engineers to build clear, accessible pages across the Stark Medical Society's CMS and member platforms.** 

Sound like you? Read on!

As our UX Engineer, you'll bring solid engineering, real curiosity, good judgment about what to test, and an interest in automation and careful use of AI tools. You'll move from discovery talks and wireframes to prototypes, production front-end code, CMS templates and proofs of concept, helping teams try ideas and ship the good ones.

**Front-End Development and CMS Delivery**
- Build and maintain responsive, accessible, fast pages across the CMS and the Society's member platforms.
- Create reusable front-end components, templates, content blocks and styles that keep the site consistent.
- Turn UX requirements, wireframes, prototypes and visual designs into clean, maintainable HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript.
- Work with backend engineers on content models, integrations, search, forms and personalization.
- Help with debugging, release checks, code reviews, documentation and modernizing older templates.

**UX and Interface Design**
- Learn who our members are, what they're trying to do, and where they get stuck.
- Draw user flows, site maps, wireframes, prototypes and interface designs sized to the project.
- Use interaction design, responsive design and information architecture to make pages easier to find, read and finish.
- Advise on layout, hierarchy, typography, imagery and calls to action within our brand.

**Prototyping, Integrations and AI**
- Build quick prototypes and proofs of concept so stakeholders can judge an idea before we invest in it.
- Connect CMS features, APIs, automation and analytics to improve pages and save staff time.
- Use AI-assisted tools responsibly for design exploration, development, documentation and testing.

**Accessibility, Quality, and Performance**
- Design and build accessibility-first: semantic markup, keyboard navigation, focus management, contrast and labeled forms.
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA and the Society's own accessibility standards.
- Run accessibility reviews, responsive and cross-browser testing, and front-end performance work.
- Track and fix defects in usability, accessibility and visual consistency with QA and content teams.

**Qualifications:**

**Required:**
- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, web development, human-computer interaction, graphic design, digital media, or a related field; or equivalent education, training, and experience.
- Three or more years of professional experience building responsive web applications, content-heavy websites, CMS-driven sites, or digital products.
- Strong proficiency in HTML5, CSS3, modern JavaScript, responsive development, and maintainable front-end code.
- Experience building reusable UI components, page templates, design patterns, or component libraries.
- Ability to turn UX/UI designs, wireframes and stakeholder feedback into finished front-end and CMS pages.
- Working knowledge of UX, visual design, information architecture, usability, and accessibility principles.
- Experience with source control, pull requests, code reviews, and issue tracking.
- Strong communication, organization, problem-solving, and prioritization skills.
- CMS template, component, or authoring-experience development; Optimizely/Episerver experience is a plus.
- Front-end depth in component-based architecture, TypeScript, Angular, React, Razor, C#, .NET, or ASP.NET Core.
- Experience using analytics, accessibility testing, WCAG 2.2 AA, Section 508, performance tuning, or Core Web Vitals to improve a site.
- Experience in a medical society, healthcare, education, nonprofit, or publishing organization.
