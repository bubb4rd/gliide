Job Description

As an Early Careers Software Engineer at Globex Bank, you'll ship code on real banking systems from your first months. You'll learn to design, build and test software alongside experienced engineers, and you'll get the coaching and room to grow into an engineer people rely on.

 

ABOUT THE PROGRAM

The Software Engineer Program is a two-year rotation for new graduates. You'll spend time on two teams, take structured courses, and have a mentor the whole way through.

You'll start with a few weeks of onboarding that cover how the bank makes money, how we build and release software, and who to call when you're stuck. 

We hire for each class on a rolling basis, so apply early — postings close once the class is full. 

 

Job Responsibilities

    Design, develop, test, and troubleshoot software solutions.

    Write and speak clearly: describe problems, document solutions, and give approved AI-assisted development tools prompts that produce good results.

    Work with engineers, managers, and stakeholders on other teams to understand problems, trade feedback, and deliver dependable solutions.
    Use approved AI development tools (for example, code generation, refactoring, test creation, and documentation) to raise quality and speed, and check their output with peer review, automated tests, and secure coding practices.

    Bring automation and modern tooling to every stage of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), including AI‑enabled tools, to make delivery faster and more reliable.

    Volunteer your skills in our Community Code days for local nonprofits.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, CAPABILITIES, AND SKILLS  

    Be pursuing a Bachelor’s degree or a 5th-year Master’s degree with an expected graduation between July 2026 and December 2026.
    Have graduated by December 2026 and be available to start full-time employment in February 2027.

    Have streams and specializations in Computer Science, Information Science, Information Technology, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence or Big Data, or related field
    Have experience designing, building, testing, and troubleshooting software as part of an engineering team.

    Write secure, high-quality code in at least one programming language, following team standards and best practices.

    Use automation and modern tooling to cut manual work across the software development life cycle.

    Use approved AI development tools (for example, code drafting, refactoring, testing, and documentation) with appropriate review.

    Judge AI-generated output critically and own the quality and correctness of what you ship.

    Follow responsible AI practices, including handling inputs and outputs securely and meeting resiliency and security expectations.

    Demonstrate strong written and verbal communication skills: explain technical ideas, document your work, and collaborate across teams and tools.

 

Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills

    Understand software engineering basics from coursework, projects, or internships.

    Have tackled open-ended problems by breaking work into steps, trying solutions, and iterating on feedback.

    Use AI development tools to speed up coding, testing, debugging, or documentation.

    Check AI-generated output carefully and own its quality, correctness, and security.

    Stay curious about new technologies and think about when they're worth using.

    Want to build technology that makes a real difference.
