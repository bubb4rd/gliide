We're hiring a Software Engineer to build the web side of a new kind of product: AI wearables that run models on the device.
 You'll build the systems behind our product site, online store, preorder launches, customer accounts, and the web companion to the device.

 What You’ll Work On 
 - Build and maintain responsive web applications with React, Next.js, TypeScript, and the modern web platform.
- Write backend services and server-side features in Node.js that hold up under launch-day traffic.
- Ship product pages, launch landing pages, checkout flows, account portals, and other storefront features.
- Work with product, design, marketing, and hardware teams to turn specs and creative ideas into finished pages.
- Connect third-party services for payments, inventory, analytics, email, support, and fulfillment.
- Make the site faster: page speed, responsiveness, search engine optimization, and conversion.
- Add analytics, attribution, A/B tests, and conversion tracking from first visit to delivery.
- Track down and fix problems anywhere in the stack to keep production healthy.
- Write down designs, decisions, and conventions so the next engineer can pick them up.

 What We’re Looking For 
 - 3+ years of professional experience in web development or software engineering.
- Strong JavaScript or TypeScript, and experience with a modern front-end framework such as React, Angular, Vue, or similar.
- Experience building server-side applications and APIs in Node.js or a similar backend framework.
- A solid grasp of REST APIs, web architecture, authentication, and integrating third-party services.
- Experience with relational or NoSQL databases.
- Knowledge of web performance work such as caching, lazy loading, server-side rendering, and image optimization.
- Daily use of Git and a modern development workflow.
- Strong problem-solving skills and patience for debugging across the stack.
- Clean, maintainable code and clear explanations of technical choices.
- Comfort owning a lot in an early-stage company that moves quickly.

 Nice to Have 
 - Deep experience with React, Next.js, and their ecosystems.
- Experience building e-commerce sites, checkout systems, preorder platforms, or crowdfunding launches.
- Familiarity with commerce platforms such as Stripe, Shopify, or similar.
- Experience with SEO, server-side rendering, accessibility, and responsive design.
- Familiarity with GraphQL, CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and DevOps.
- Experience with analytics and experimentation tools.
- Interest in AI products, consumer hardware, and new technology.
