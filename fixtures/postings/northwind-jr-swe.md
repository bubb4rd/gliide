About Northwind Media

 Northwind Media makes the messaging, video and reminder tools that community clinics use to stay in touch with their patients between visits. Clinic staff use our products to send a refill reminder in Spanish, share a short care video after a diagnosis, or check in with a family that missed an appointment.

 We care about a few things above the rest. We ship small and often, because a feature in a nurse's hands teaches us more than a feature in a spec. We ask the people who use our tools what they need, and we change course when the answer surprises us. We measure what we build, and we say so when it didn't work.

 If that sounds like the kind of place you want to learn the craft, we'd like to hear from you.

 About this role

 Engineers at Northwind Media build for clinic staff — front-desk coordinators, nurses, health educators and outreach workers — who talk to thousands of patients a week.

 As a Junior Software Engineer, you'll join a product team with a senior engineer as your mentor and pick up real tickets from your first week. You'll write code, review code, and learn how our services talk to each other. We're looking for someone curious who isn't shy about asking why — we'll put real time into your growth while you do work that reaches patients.

 This role is meant for someone in the first year or two of their engineering career.

 You don't need to know Elixir yet — we'll teach you. 

 What you'll do in your first six months

- Ship features and fixes to our patient messaging service, written in Elixir

- Pick up bug tickets anywhere in our stack (React, TypeScript, Elixir)

- Get a local development environment running that matches production

- Join sprint planning and refinement, and track your work in Jira

- Take part in code reviews on GitHub and share your progress at standup

- Plan upcoming features with senior engineers, product and design

- Query and migrate PostgreSQL, our main production database

 Qualifications

- Some experience building software — a job, an internship, a bootcamp, or a sizable side project all count

- Familiarity with at least one modern web application framework (e.g. React, Next.js, Phoenix, or similar)

- Familiarity with Git and version control workflows

- Strong written and verbal communication skills

 Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with functional programming

- Familiarity with testing frameworks and CI/CD pipelines

- Interest in healthcare technology and patient communication

- Comfort with the pace and ambiguity of a small, mission-driven company
