# Software Engineer, ROS

Pittsburgh, PA, United StatesEarly CareerID: 31842

Apply Now

[Back to search results](https://careers.acme.example/jobs)

Acme Robotics builds the warehouse robots that pick, pack and sort for thousands of distribution centers– and the software fleet that keeps them moving.

At Acme Robotics, we think the best robots are the ones nobody has to think about. We want every shift lead to trust the fleet, every technician to fix a robot in minutes, and every engineer to ship without fear.We’re growing fast, and we’re looking for people who like hard problems and plain answers.

Working here means writing software that moves real things, solving problems that only show up at scale, and helping a young company grow up without slowing down.

As a Software Engineer on the ROS (Robotics Operations Systems) team, you'll build the internal products the company runs on. ROS owns the tools and data that describe how Acme Robotics works — who is on which team, which robots are in which building, and which projects are moving — through products like Fleet Ledger, Crew Directory, Site Maps, and Project Board. As we bring AI into more of our work, you'll make that data accurate and easy to reach for people and AI agents alike.

**You Will**
- Partner with engineering, operations, finance, and people teams to find the internal tools worth building, and write down what they need.
- Get teams to agree on what a product should do and keep them pointed the same way.
- Own projects from first sketch to launch, planned well and built to last.
- Move between frontend and backend work, data pipelines and infrastructure, picking up whatever the problem needs.
- Hold a high bar for code quality, building systems that stay fast as the company grows.
- Try AI tools and agents in your own work and keep the ones that pay off.

**
You Have**
- 1-3 years of experience in software engineering (internships count), with production code you're proud of.
- **Coding Proficiency:** Strong programming skills in at least one modern language (e.g., TypeScript, Python), with experience in a frontend framework such as React.
- **Problem-Solving Skills:** Solid computer science fundamentals, data structures, and algorithms, and patience for debugging hard issues.
- **Communication**: You work well with engineers and with partners who don't write code
- **Education: **A Bachelor's degree (or equivalent professional experience) in Computer Science or a related engineering field.
