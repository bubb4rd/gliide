 If you nodded three times, keep reading. 

 Vandelay Realty & Development, LLC manages about 1,200 rental units for several hundred owners across Chicago's western suburbs. We've grown every year since 2009, and we want to keep going. We're hiring a Systems & Automation Engineer to build the automations, integrations and in-house tools our teams use every day, and to keep the office and its devices working. 

 Role Overview 

 We want to manage five times as many units without five times the staff, and this job is how we get there. Roughly 85% of your week goes to building web applications, Podio workflows, SQL reports, API integrations and Python or JavaScript automations for our maintenance, leasing, property management and accounting teams. The other 15% is hands-on IT: setting up laptops and phones, managing user access, and keeping the office running. You'll work closely with our Operations Analyst, who decides what the business needs, while you decide how to build it, with priorities set by our owner. This is a fully onsite role at our Elmhurst, IL office. 

 What You Get 

- Salary: $78,000 to $100,000 per year, based on experience 

- Quarterly Bonus: Performance based, paid every quarter 

- Health Coverage: Health, dental, and vision, with most of the employee premium paid 

- Paid Time Off: 2 weeks PTO in your first year 

- Paid Holidays: 8 paid holidays a year 

- 401(k): Retirement plan available 

- Learning Budget: Up to $500 a year for books and courses 

 What You'll Do 

- Build Automations: Create workflows in Podio, n8n, and custom scripts that take hours of manual work off our teams each week 

- Develop Web Applications: Build and improve our internal web applications, like the owner portal and the tenant text-message center 

- Manage Data and Integrations: Keep data moving between Podio, AppFolio, MySQL, and Supabase, and fix breaks within one business day 

- Turn Ideas Into Solutions: Work with our Operations Analyst to turn requests into plans, then build, test, and ship them 

- Own User Access: Grant and remove access in Google Workspace, our password manager, and our business systems the day someone joins or leaves 

- Support Devices: Set up and manage laptops and Android phones, and fix routine problems within four hours 

- Keep the Office Running: Look after our server, network, printers, internet, and phone system 

- Protect Our Data: Enforce MFA, watch backups, install security updates, and document every system so anyone can pick it up 

 What We're Looking For 

- Coding Ability: Working Python or JavaScript, including calling REST APIs, handling webhooks, and moving data between systems 

- Hands On IT: Willing to set up, troubleshoot, and repair laptops, phones, printers, and network gear yourself 

- SQL Skills: Comfortable querying, joining, and modeling data in MySQL and Postgres 

- Web Development: Experience building or maintaining web applications; React or Next.js a strong plus 

- Low Code Tools: Familiarity with Podio, n8n, Zapier, or similar, and good sense about when they're the simpler answer 

- AI Coding Tools: Comfortable using AI tools to build faster 

- Security Discipline: Understanding of user access, SSO, MFA, and password managers 

- Clear Communication: Able to explain technical tradeoffs in plain language and push back when a request doesn't make sense 

- Experience: 2+ years in a technical role involving scripting, integrations, or systems administration 

- Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field, or equivalent hands on experience 

- Physical Requirements: Able to lift up to 50 lbs and work under desks or in equipment rooms 

- Location: Fully onsite in Elmhurst, IL, five days a week, and able to pass a background check
