Initech Consulting https://www.initech.example is an employee-owned technology consulting firm working with clients across North America. We help companies plan, build and run the software their business depends on, from a first prototype to a system that serves millions. Read more https://www.initech.example/careers/ about how we work, how we hire, and how to prepare for our interviews.

Want to start your career on a small team that will push you, teach you, and have your back?

Initech Consulting hires a class of new graduates every year and puts them on client work from the start. We're looking for people who like solving problems with technology and like helping the people around them do the same.

To see what we value day to day, read our Working Agreements https://www.initech.example/careers/#agreements – the habits we hold each other to.



You Can Expect To:

As an Initech Consultant, you'll join a small project team and work side by side with client staff on problems that matter to their business.

 - Grow with a dedicated mentor and a written career path. Reviews come every 6 months, and promotions can come every 12.

 - Do a bit of everything: estimate work, gather requirements, build, test, run projects, design systems and ship them.

 - Work across many technologies: Cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, Data Engineering with Databricks and PySpark, and DevOps practices such as CI/CD, Docker, and Terraform. Build AI and Generative AI features with RAG, LLMs, prompt engineering, and agent frameworks like LangChain, writing Python, Java, SQL, JavaScript, and .NET, and building web front ends with React and Angular

 - Use AI tools to deliver faster and find insights, and explain to clients plainly what AI can and can't do and how to use it responsibly.

 - Build your communication skills by presenting findings, designs and demos to client leaders.

 - Work within a hybrid model.

 - Take part in learning sessions, volunteer days and team events.

 - Work with kind, curious, capable people.



We Look For:

 - Technologists with software development or relevant internship experience

 - People who keep learning long after school ends

 - Problem solvers who have built something new

 - Adaptable people who enjoy new clients, new teams and new technologies

 - Future technology leaders who want to grow quickly

 - Clear communicators who can explain ideas to technical and non-technical audiences

 - Currently pursuing a bachelor's or master's degree in Management Information Systems (MIS), Computer Science, Computer Engineering or comparable field with an anticipated graduation date between December 2026 and August 2027

 - Legally authorized to work for any company in the United States without current or future sponsorship
