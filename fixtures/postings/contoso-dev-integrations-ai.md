### Description

**Position Summary**

The Developer - Integrations & AI keeps Contoso Learning’s business systems, partner platforms and in-house AI services talking to each other. With senior developers, technical leads and outside development partners, you’ll write the APIs, scripts and web application code that move data between our applications and approved AI tools, and you’ll extend the customizations in our core business platforms. You’ll add AI-enabled features on top of patterns the team already has, not build the framework underneath, and learn applied AI integration on the job.

**Responsibilities**
-  Write and maintain web application, integration and automation code in JavaScript, Python, and PHP, including the scripts and services behind AI-enabled features and data preparation.
-  Build and maintain integrations between internal systems and outside resources: partner and vendor platforms, third-party APIs, and hosted services.
- Act as a technical contact for outside developers, vendors and partners: agree on requirements, review what they deliver, and bring it into our systems.
-  Connect business applications to our internal AI services and approved AI tools using the patterns the team has set.
- Support and extend customizations, workflows and settings in our core business platforms.
- Work with technical leads, Business Intelligence and business users to turn requests into working solutions.
- Test, debug and document integrations, customizations and AI-enabled features.
- Troubleshoot problems with business applications, workflows, integrations and connected services.
-  Follow our version control and change control practices, and keep process, code and user documentation current.
-  Take on other support and development work as assigned.
-  Keep up with languages, integration patterns and new practices through training and mentorship.

**Education & Skill Requirements**
-  Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field; relevant coursework or equivalent practical experience accepted.
- Working knowledge of JavaScript and the web platform, and of Python for scripting, automation and integration work; PHP experience also counts.
- Familiarity with web development concepts such as HTML, CSS, JSON, and client/server architecture, and experience with REST APIs and third-party services.
-  Some exposure to AI or LLM-enabled development, such as calling model APIs, writing prompts, or using an internal AI service.
- Experience with version control tools (e.g., Git).
- Experience customizing enterprise business platforms such as Oracle NetSuite is a plus but not required, as is basic SQL or relational database knowledge.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
-  Ability to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical colleagues, vendors and partners.
-  Enjoys learning new technologies and solving problems with a team.
